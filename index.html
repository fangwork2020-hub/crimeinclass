What happened

After the City saw the robbery hotspot at the Train Station, it added extra police patrols around the station starting in week 5. The patrol zone is the blue dashed area on the map.

The patrols stayed on the City Police side of the line. Nothing else changed in the neighborhood.

Time

Layers

Model A. Uses: five years of arrest records. Suggests: more patrols at the station and in Harbor Flats.
Model B. Uses: streetlight outage reports, foot traffic measured from people’s phone location data, and station exits. Suggests: repair lights on Mill Alley, share alerts with the Sheriff, put an attendant at the north exit.
1

Look

Click weeks 1–4 (before extra patrols), then weeks 5–8 (after extra patrols). Switch back and forth a few times.

What changed at the Train Station? What changed somewhere else on the map? Compare the three counts. The City says its patrols worked. Do you agree?

2

Explain

Turn on streetlights.

Why did robbery show up where it did in weeks 5–8? Who is responsible for that area? What would you do next if you were the City, and what if you were the Sheriff?

3

Decide

Turn on Model A, then Model B. Read what each one uses and suggests.

If the city could buy only one, which would you choose? What does each model get right, and what does each risk? Is there a version you’d trust more than either?
